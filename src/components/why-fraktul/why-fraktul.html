Why it exists

Understanding a system is now the hard part

I have wanted this tool for years. I kept needing it in the same three situations: knowledge-transfer sessions when I took over a project, teaching a design to teams as an architect, and having to grasp other people's services and systems so I could build new ones beside them.

Each time, what I needed was in someone's head, or in a diagram. And we all know diagrams go out of date almost as soon as they are drawn.

Now AI writes most of the code, and someone still has to understand it.

fraktul is the tool I wanted: one map that goes from the big picture down to the exact code, that can explain even a complex system to someone non-technical, and that says so when it has gone out of date.

The maker of fraktul, Rinsesoft

  • 01

    Taking over a project

    The handover is a tour you can replay: the map, the person's voice, and every click they made, in sync.

  • 02

    Teaching a design to a team

    Draw the design once. Each person clicks into the part they own and lands on its real files and functions.

  • 03

    Learning someone else's system

    Start from the whole and click down until you reach a line of source. Who calls whom is read from the code, so it is never out of date.

  • 04

    Keeping up with what AI writes

    New modules show up as not mapped yet, and a moved file breaks the check. The picture in your head and the code in the repo stay the same thing.
