Design system

Two styles share one set of tokens, each in light and dark. Flat is the default: a quiet modern UI for everyday work. Y2K is the fun one, opt-in: a liquid-chrome nav, wide Michroma titles, a holographic foil logo and brushed-metal diagram boxes, with modern glass controls. Components only read tokens, so both always work.

Rules

  1. Readability first. Body text always sits on a flat surface (--sheet or --paper). Every text colour passes WCAG AA on it; the swatches above check it live.
  2. Material is for the frame, not content. Gradients appear on the nav bar, buttons, chips, the logo and the tour strip. Never behind a paragraph.
  3. One signal colour. --hi means "look here": the tour highlight and the narration's current line. Nothing else uses it.
  4. Colour says who does the work. Diagram boxes: a model, code, a person, data. The border carries the colour; fills stay quiet.
  5. Display type is for names. Michroma for titles and labels in y2k; body text is Manrope (y2k) or Inter (flat).
  6. Motion is optional. The foil shimmer and floating art stop for anyone who prefers reduced motion.
