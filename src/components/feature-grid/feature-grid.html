One file in, a whole fraktul out

You write the blocks and how they connect. Everything below a block, the files, the functions, who calls whom and the source, fraktul reads from the code at every build.

  • Click all the way down

    Every box with a badge opens into its own diagram: systems, services, features, files, functions, then the source, with each call a link.

  • Peek before you jump

    Hover a box to see its summary and a small copy of what's inside. One click to go down, one to come back up.

  • Tours that follow the data

    Step through a flow, one order or one release, with branches where the path splits and shortcuts to dive in.

  • Narrated in a real voice

    Write what to say; a neural voice speaks it on your own Mac. The fraktul highlights each part as the narration reaches it.

  • Record your own walkthrough

    Talk while you click. Your voice and every step you take are saved together and replay in sync for the next person.

  • Checked against the code

    A moved file, a renamed function or a stale tour step fails the check in CI, and new modules show up as not mapped yet.
