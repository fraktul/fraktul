# Code of conduct

fraktul is a small project and we want it to be a decent place to work.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons.
- Welcome newcomers. A first question or a first pull request is how every contributor started.
- Accept that maintainers decide what goes in, and that "no" or "not now" is not personal.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately disrupting issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone is speaking for the
project.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainers privately: open the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/fraktul/fraktul/security) and choose **Report a vulnerability**, marking the
report as a conduct concern. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock
threads, and block people who do not follow this code.
